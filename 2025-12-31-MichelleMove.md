I live with my romantic partner Michelle now! She got a new job and decided to move out of her parent's place and move in with me! We found this tremendous loft that we're so very lucky to have. It has one proper bedroom, and a lofted room that would only be a good bedroom for a monk or an exhibitionist. From the loft you can see the rest of the apartment, which includes our living room and a kitchen that looks like a gallery installation of a kitchen. BP is much happier with all of the vertical space, and with having multiple partners who dote on him all the time. 

A week or so ago we held a moving in/housewarming party, and we got to see our new home filled with beloved friends. When we laid out our furniture we tried to make it so that the space would have all these little pockets for interaction, and that was successful! It was actually hard to get everyone's attention together for the white elephant. What a wonderful thing.
